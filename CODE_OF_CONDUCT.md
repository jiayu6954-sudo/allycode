# Code of Conduct

AllyCode is committed to a respectful, constructive, and technically rigorous
community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful of different backgrounds, experience levels, and viewpoints.
- Discuss ideas and code without personal attacks.
- Give actionable feedback and assume good intent while still addressing risks.
- Protect user privacy and disclose security concerns through private channels.
- Credit other people's work and respect applicable licenses.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Publishing another person's private information or credentials.
- Deliberately introducing malicious code, hidden telemetry, or unsafe instructions.
- Repeatedly disrupting discussions or ignoring maintainer moderation.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close contributions, or temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior conflicts with this policy.
Serious security or safety concerns should be reported privately using the process in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

When reporting conduct concerns, include relevant context and links. Reports will be
reviewed as confidentially as practical.
